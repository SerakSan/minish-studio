Windows & Mac controls

On Mac, Command is the ⌘ key and Option is the ⌥ key. Windows uses Ctrl and Alt. Buttons work with a mouse or trackpad on both systems.

Keyboard and placement shortcuts
ActionWindowsMac

Animation

Choose Animate with the Ezlo switch. Simple lets you choose an action, movement and speed for each piece, then drag it into place. Play previews every piece together. Settings controls length in seconds. Add Dialogue and choose Reveal text for a ready text effect. Moving a piece adjusts its whole path.

Timeline gives you individual poses, keys and timing. Switching views preserves the project. Custom animation remains intact in Simple until you explicitly choose Replace this piece’s animation. Animation and Scene have separate projects, saves and Undo. Import current scene creates an editable copy.

Animation workspace shortcuts
ActionWindowsMac

Use the timeline scrubber or frame field to move precisely. Add key, Delete key, Copy key, Paste key, and the previous/next key buttons edit timing. In the collection, choose genuine poses and set Frames per pose before Create pose sequence. Quick motion adds position keys. Lock prevents edits to a track; Mute hides its piece during playback and export. Visible can be keyed to make a piece appear or disappear. Previous frame shows an editing guide and is excluded from exports.

Use Export animation for GIF, PNG frames, a sprite sheet, or browser-supported video. PNG keeps exact pixels and transparency. GIF has a 256-color palette, and supported video formats depend on the browser. Frame limits, progress, and Cancel are shown before or during export.

Delete or reuse pieces: Delete and Backspace work from the canvas and the Pieces list. Copy, Cut, Paste, and Duplicate use Ctrl on Windows or Command on Mac. Copying stays inside the editor. The action bar beside the canvas provides buttons too.

Delete a complete canvas: Delete scene beside the scene tabs removes its entire canvas. Delete animation beside the animation tabs removes all pieces and timing in that clip. If it is the last one, a blank canvas remains in the same format. One Undo restores everything. Ctrl/Command+Shift+Delete or Backspace is the shortcut when you are not typing.

Mouse and trackpad

  • Choose and place: click a collection piece to choose a brush, double-click to place it in the center, or drag it into the workspace. A drag holds its center; the preview shows grid alignment.
  • Build and arrange: Paint stamps copies, Select moves pieces, and Erase removes whole pieces from the active layer. Off-frame pieces stay editable and are cropped from exports.
  • Scroll and zoom: use the workspace scrollbars, mouse wheel, or two-finger trackpad scrolling. Pinch with two fingers over the canvas to zoom around the pointer. Ctrl+mouse wheel also zooms. Ordinary two-finger scrolling pans. Use +/−, 0 for actual size, or F to fit; the visible zoom buttons work too. Focus opens more workspace.
  • Keyboard access: Tab and Shift+Tab move focus. Enter or Space activates focused buttons. In library tabs, Left/Right and Home/End change the collection window. On Mac, enable your browser's keyboard navigation if Tab skips controls.
  • Precise changes: X/Y inputs and nudge buttons are in Settings. Shift with a nudge button also moves one grid step. The 1 px precision button is available when a modifier key is inconvenient.

Scene, art, and file controls

Pieces and Settings open the side panels on smaller screens. Settings puts selected-piece controls first. Canvas & background contains scene dimensions, format and transparency. Layers, scale, flips, order, duplication, deletion, and dialogue remain editable. The carousel adds, duplicates, and switches scenes. The collection controls sources, pose groups, directions, and tile size; Change selected pose and Edit boss parts act on a placed piece. Source archive crops a source sheet, My crops keeps saved selections, and PNG ↓ downloads one piece. Save project keeps editable JSON, Open restores it, Export PNG saves the frame, and Export all PNGs saves the carousel.

Random scene opens the settings. Surprise me again changes an existing generated scene in one click, with Undo available. When a handmade scene is active, it adds a new scene.

While typing or adjusting a field, text editing and field navigation keep their usual behavior. Save and Open remain available. Scene shortcuts pause while a dialog is open; Esc closes the dialog. Right-click and Mac Control-click keep the browser's context menu.

 A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Make a world worth sharing.

  1. Find your pieces. Open Environments, Characters, Boss parts, Objects or Enemies. Full bosses contains editor assemblies made from genuine parts, grouped by state. Select one in the scene and choose Edit boss parts to adjust its components. Choose a source, then click any available pose or tile. Place it in the scene, or paint with your selected brush.
  2. Try another pose. Select a character in your scene, pick another pose from its source, then choose “Change selected pose.” You can flip, move, resize and layer every piece.
  3. Build your scene. Paint onto the canvas. Use Terrain for the ground, Objects for props, and Overlay for foreground details. Select lets you move placed pieces, including large map backgrounds.
  4. Make it personal. Add a dialogue box, type your own words, flip pieces or adjust their scale. Add more scenes for a carousel.
  5. Choose your format. Use a social preset or open Settings and select Free format. Set the width, height and PNG enlargement, then Apply size.
  6. Keep it crisp. Export a whole scene as PNG, or use PNG ↓ to save one transparent piece. Save project keeps every scene editable and portable.

Exact placement: drag from the collection to hold a piece by its center. Turn on 1 px precision, hold Alt / Option to bypass the grid, or use X/Y and the nudge buttons in Settings. Faded artwork outside the frame stays editable and is cropped from exports.

Surprise me: Random scene combines 48 stories across 99 genuine game environments. Choose Game-inspired adventures, Wild mashups, or either, plus a mood and cast size. Surprise me again rerolls a generated scene in one click; Undo restores it. Scenes made from scratch are kept.

Large maps: choose a clean map region in Environments and press “Place in scene.” It starts at its original size. Switch to Select and drag the map to frame another area.

Manual crop: open Source archive to see the unchanged sheet. It may include community captions or palettes. Select only the game artwork, then save it to My crops. Exact color removal is optional and affects only new pieces.

Saving: your project autosaves in this browser. Save project also downloads a portable JSON file. Keep this app folder and its assets together.

Pixel accuracy: all supplied sheet artwork is preserved. A free crop is often best for sprites. A source sheet's labels or alignment gaps can be excluded by selecting the artwork precisely.
