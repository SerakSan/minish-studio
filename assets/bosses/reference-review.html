Complete boss reference review

Minish Studio by SerakSan. Available published complete frames and reference-informed editable multipart presets for all nine boss sheets. Continuous affine transforms, joint curves, hidden ROM states and exact animation timelines are not exhaustively reconstructed. No community labels or example collages are included as boss artwork.

Every preset can be separated into its genuine source parts in the editor. The links below open independent captured gameplay or geometry research for comparison. They are references, not copied artwork in this pack.

Big Green Chuchu

Six upright head expressions on the four-part standing arrangement checked against Nintendo gameplay, at normal 1.6× affine scale. All 27 large head states remain available in Boss parts. Falling and defeat expressions are not attached to an unsupported standing body; exact collapse curves and ROM timing are not reconstructed.

Big Blue Chuchu

Six upright head expressions with genuine narrow neck and wide foot, using the shared Chuchu four-part geometry and normal 1.6× affine scale. All 27 head states and electric effects remain in Boss parts. Falling, defeat curves and the ROM timeline are not reconstructed.

Big Octorok

Normal, burning and frozen source states; four separate legs, mouth, four tail joints and tail flower; eight rotation presets. Body uses the game’s normal 1.6× affine scale. Rotation, breathing and tail movement vary continuously, so these are reference-informed editor poses.

Gleerok

Genuine rocky body, five neck links and distinct jawed head. Front, side, lowered and damaged arrangements use the available directional source frames. Neck curves are editable presets; exact ROM animation timings and every curve are not asserted.
